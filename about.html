Rachel Parker is a designer specialising in incorporating play and mixed media, embracing character and humor, as well as finding unique solutions to design problems. During her time studying at the National College of Art and Design in Dublin, she worked on several design projects, including design identity NCAD Zooms-In talks, submissions for the D&AD New Blood awards and book covers for Penguin. She studied abroad at the ArtEz University in Arnhem, where she had the opportunity to work alongside other design students from all across the world, including designing magazines for the Eflux archive among other collaborative projects. She was commissioned to design a label for Pickhandle, a distillery located in Co. Limerick, for their new Kombucha product range. She has experience in sales at several design markets and events and also has worked in an archival role organising art and design books in the private library of an art historian.
